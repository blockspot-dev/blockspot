


Installing directions
--

1. Go to https://download.geofabrik.de/north-america/us-northeast.html and download us-northeast-latest.osm.pbf
2. Move us-northeast-latest.osm.pbf to C:\blockspot\data\osrm and rename to us-northeast-latest.osm.pbf
3. in cmd, run 

docker run -t -v "C:/blockspot/data/osrm:/data" ghcr.io/project-osrm/osrm-backend osrm-extract -ddp /opt/car.lua /data/us-northeast-latest.osm.pbf
